MyMediaLog privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

MyMediaLog is a personal log for books, films, series and other media. This policy explains what happens to the information you put in it.

The app does not collect, send or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it does not use the internet.

Everything you add (titles, creators such as authors or directors, types, statuses, dates, ratings, progress and notes) is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. The developer can't see it.

If Android backup is turned on in your device's settings, Android may copy this app's data, including your notes, to your Google account, under Google's terms. The developer can't see or access that copy. You can turn this off in your device's backup settings. It isn't a substitute for Export backup.

When you use Export backup, the app writes a file to the place you choose, such as your device's storage or a cloud drive. That file is yours: it goes only where you save it, and the developer never receives it.

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Deleted items are kept for up to 30 days so they can be restored, then erased. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage erases all of its data from the device. A copy in your Google account backup is managed by Google under your account settings.

The app is not directed at children under 13.
